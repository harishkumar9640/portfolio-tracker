{# Inline glossary for non-experts #}
What do these terms mean?
Promoter
The people/entities who founded or control the company (e.g. the Ambani family for Reliance). 0% means the company is "professionally managed" with no single controlling owner.
FII
Foreign Institutional Investors — overseas funds (BlackRock, Vanguard, Norwegian sovereign wealth fund, etc.) that hold Indian shares.
DII
Domestic Institutional Investors — Indian institutions like mutual funds, banks, insurance companies. (DII = MF + Banks + Insurance + Other DI.)
Mutual Funds
Indian mutual fund schemes that hold this stock.
Banks
Indian banks holding this stock.
Insurance
Indian insurance companies (LIC, etc.) holding this stock.
Public
Retail investors and other non-institutional holders.
Others
Trusts, ESOPs, foreign bodies corporate, etc. that don't fit the above buckets.
Pledged
Shares the promoter has pledged as collateral for a loan. High pledged % is a red flag (risk of forced selling).
