How these models work

Graham Number
√(22.5 × EPS × BVPS) — Benjamin Graham's classic intrinsic-value formula. Conservative; useful as a sanity check.
PE-Relative
EPS × industry PE — values a stock at the average PE of its peers. Requires an industry-PE input.
Two-stage DCF
PV of FCF/share for 5 years (growing at g₁) plus a discounted terminal value (growing at g₂). Defaults: g₁=10%, g₂=3%, r=10%.
Margin
(fair_value − price) / price. Positive = undervalued; negative = overvalued. The badge colour follows the sign.
