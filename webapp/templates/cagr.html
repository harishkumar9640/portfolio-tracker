Method & data quality

How this is computed
  • Lot reconstruction: We read the cumulative Open Holdings at each year-end across your 5 tax P&L xlsx files (FY-end snapshots at 2023-03-31, 2024-03-31, 2025-03-31, 2026-03-31, and 2026-07-04 for the partial current FY). For each successive snapshot, the qty change = net buys in that FY. We subtract intra-FY sells (from each year's Delivery P&L) to figure out the buy lot. Each lot is dated at the FY midpoint (Oct 1 for an Apr-Mar FY).
  • FIFO matching: All Delivery P&L sells are applied to the earliest lot first. So the earliest lot of a ticker shrinks when you sell, and replacement buys (implied by flat cumulative position) create new lots at later dates.
  • Lot price: For each still-open lot, the buy price is backed out from the cumulative value change between snapshots, after accounting for the cost basis of any sells (from Delivery P&L). For the oldest lot, the price = the cumulative avg at the first snapshot where it's the only remaining lot. For newer lots, the price is solved from the cumulative value change. Delivery P&L exact buy prices are used where the buy dates match.
  • Per-stock CAGR: (LTP / avg_price) ^ (1 / years) − 1, where years = days from lot's buy date to today ÷ 365.25.
  • Nifty50 CAGR (per-lot): same formula, using the Nifty50 close (^NSEI) on the lot's buy date vs today. Source: data/cache/indices_cache.csv (4,609 trading days, Sep 2007 → today).
  • Nifty50 CAGR (from first buy date): the apples-to-apples benchmark. Nifty50 close on your earliest open-lot buy date (typically your first ever investment) vs today, as a single CAGR. Compared against your portfolio's invested-weighted CAGR, this is the fairest head-to-head.
  • Aggregate: invested-weighted average of years held across all reliable lots, then total return ÷ weighted years. Excludes lots with < 30 days held (unreliable_buy_date flag).
  • Source labels: first_seen = ticker first appeared in this xlsx's Open Holdings (so this is the earliest lot we can infer); snapshot_diff = the position grew between two snapshots, so a buy must have happened in the FY; manual / manual_override = from MANUAL_BUY_DATES (UNOMINDA, BALRAMCHIN, NEXT50IETF); reconciliation = fallback lot to make reconstructed qty match the truth file (when our reconstruction was off).
  • Limitations: lot buy dates are FY midpoints for older holdings (±~3 months uncertainty). Per-lot buy prices are backed out from the cumulative-avg change between snapshots and cross-checked against Delivery P&L where possible. For very old pre-2024 holdings where Delivery P&L has no per-lot data, the price is the cumulative avg at the next snapshot (where the lot is the only one remaining). The 2026-27 xlsx has a broker-template bug ("as of 29/06/2027") which we clamp to today.
