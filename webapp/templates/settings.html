📧 Mutual Fund Alerts

Every day at 16:30 IST (just after market close) the server checks Trendlyne for changes in MF holdings of your 8 stocks. If anything changed, you'll get an email with a per-stock diff. If you have no SMTP credentials configured, the alert runs in "dry-run" mode and just logs the email body.

SMTP setup

Set these env vars (or add them to secrets.local.json):

  • MF_ALERT_SMTP_HOST e.g. smtp.gmail.com
  • MF_ALERT_SMTP_PORT e.g. 587
  • MF_ALERT_SMTP_USER your full email address
  • MF_ALERT_SMTP_PASS app password (Gmail users: create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)
  • MF_ALERT_TO recipient (defaults to SMTP_USER)
  • MF_ALERT_DRY_RUN 1 to log instead of send

Disable the scheduler with MF_ALERT_DISABLED=1 when starting the server.
